## 2. The service

Aside provides:

- **Live answer mode** — captures your microphone and optionally the meeting audio, transcribes it with Apple Speech Recognition on your Mac or with AssemblyAI streaming, detects questions, and generates suggested spoken answers grounded in the CV you uploaded
- **Resume + Cover Letter generator** — produces a tailored CV and cover letter for a specific job description, grounded in your base CV
- **History** — stores past sessions and generated documents for your later review
- **Profile management** — multiple CVs (one active at a time)
